# Closed-beta launch gates

Real-money operation remains disabled until every owner signs off.

## Legal and compliance

- Philippine counsel classifies donation and reward flows, campaign terms, prohibited campaigns, refund rights, complaints, and future securities boundaries.
- Privacy officer completes the data inventory, privacy impact assessment, retention schedule, vendor agreements, access review, and breach workflow.
- Accountant confirms platform-fee recognition, processor fees, withholding, receipts/invoices, refunds, chargebacks, and campaign payout reporting.
- Marketing removes unprovable trust, guarantee, escrow, investment, and outcome claims.

## Providers

- PayMongo approves PitchPond’s platform account, sub-merchant onboarding, all-or-nothing holding/transfer behavior, refund timing, dispute workflow, limits, and supported channels.
- Sumsub or the selected verification provider confirms Philippine document coverage, retention, data location, webhook security, manual-review escalation, and deletion terms.
- Email/SMS sender identities and domains are verified; support can reach users during payment and security incidents.

## Product and operations

- Invite-only limits, founder/backer caps, transaction caps, manual payout hold, and emergency feature flags are configured.
- Every campaign has a verified accountable entity, approved payout account, specific budget, risk disclosure, fulfilment plan, and review audit event.
- Support owns response targets for failed payments, refunds, fulfilment complaints, account recovery, privacy requests, and suspected fraud.
- Sandbox reconciliation shows zero duplicate charges/payouts and no unexplained balance difference.

## Engineering release gate

- API and web tests, lint, typecheck, production builds, dependency audit, authorization tests, accessibility checks, backup restore, and rollback rehearsal pass.
- Monitoring covers HTTP errors, login spikes, webhook failures, stale payments, queue depth, campaign deadlines, refunds, payouts, and database capacity.
